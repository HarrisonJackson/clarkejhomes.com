Eternal outsider Ayla left the faith community that raised her as a caravan of roving
trailers. She returns as the wife of the leader's beloved son. She finds it's become an
international megachurch where everyone's bags are designer and sin doesn't even begin
to cover it.

Ayla moves to the faith's exclusive island community with her husband and infant
daughter. But just as she settles into the happily ever after part of her Cinderella
story, two corpses crash their lavish homecoming party. Told never to question God's
plan, the community moves on easily from the horrific discovery, but Ayla's anxiety
spirals.

To bring her back to the faith's central tenet of acceptance, Ayla agrees to therapy and
drug treatments managed by the church. As the treatments blur the lines of her reality,
a face she cannot place, a husband she no longer recognizes, and cryptic notes from her
late mother push her toward a dangerous suspicion. Her church has a secret, and it's
been killing its own followers to keep it.

Ayla can trust her instincts and risk her family, security, and faith, or she can shut
her eyes, keep silent, and enjoy her life of privilege.

*If the only thing that will satisfy your appetite is to eat the rich, add this to your
reading menu.*
