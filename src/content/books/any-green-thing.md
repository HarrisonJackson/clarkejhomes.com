Luxury is anything but quiet in The Enclave. This exclusive island community is home to
the elite of a growing megachurch, and a new family is moving in.

Ayla isn’t accustomed to a life of privilege but knows The Enclave is where God and her
late mother want her to be. But a face she cannot place, a husband she no longer
recognizes, and cryptic notes left behind by her late mother force Ayla to ask a
dangerous question.

Is she there to enjoy her new, lavish life, or to uncover the rot beneath it?

Set in the Florida Keys, full of twists and suspense, this novel is a reminder that
survival can depend upon trusting your instincts and trusting yourself.

*If the only thing that satisfies your appetite is eating the rich, add this to your
reading menu.*
